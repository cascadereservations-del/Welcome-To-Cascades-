Beyond the City: Nearby Excursions

🚗 Getting there: These destinations lie outside General Santos City and require your own arrangements — Grab (where available), a rented vehicle, or a van hire from terminal. Travel times below are approximate from Cascade Hideaway. Factor in an early start for the best experience.

🏖️

Gumasa Beach

Glan, Sarangani Province

Boracay of the South White Sand

Six kilometres of powdery white sand facing the Celebes Sea — calm, uncrowded, and genuinely beautiful. Resorts along the strip offer day visits and overnight stays. Best experienced on a dry-season morning before the midday heat.

Getting There

Van from KCC Mall terminal → Glan (~1 hr), then tricycle to beach

Best Time

Feb–April dry season · Morning visits · Last van back by 4 PM

📍 Navigate to Gumasa
🌊

Lake Sebu

South Cotabato Highlands

Seven Falls Zipline T'boli Culture

A highland escape at 1,000m elevation — cool air, mist-covered mountains, and one of Southeast Asia's highest ziplines soaring over seven cascading waterfalls. Add a lake cruise, T'boli Dreamweavers weaving, and fresh tilapia lunch for a genuinely full day.

Getting There

Bus from Bulaong Terminal → ~2 hrs · Habal-habal in Lake Sebu

Don't Miss

Zipline ₱350/person · Lotus flowers bloom 5:30–8 AM only

📍 Navigate to Lake Sebu
🍓

SG Farm

Strawberry & Guyabano · Tupi, South Cotabato

#1 Most Visited Mt. Matutum View

Perched on the slopes overlooking Mt. Matutum, this mountaintop farm resort offers cool highland air, strawberry and guyabano orchards, and stunning valley views. Overnight glamping available — a peaceful escape that's ranked the most-visited attraction in South Cotabato Province.

🚕 Grab · approx. 45 min from property

📍 Navigate to SG Farm →
🌸

Euphoria Flower Farm & Adventure Park

Flower Fields & Rides · Tupi, South Cotabato

Golden Hour Family Friendly

Golden hour at the ridge

1 / 2

Flower fields and viewing decks along the ridge, the whole valley falling away beneath. Arrive late afternoon and stay — the hour before sunset turns the fields gold and the far ranges blue. Camping is available if you would rather not hand the view back.

Good to Know

Entrance, rides and camping are each priced separately and change with the season and with promos. Check their official site for today's rates before you set off.

Before you go: the final access road is steep and bumpy — genuinely slippery after rain. A high-clearance vehicle is the comfortable choice.

Planning Your GenSan Experience

🌅 Morning

Start early at the Fish Port (5–6 AM) to witness the tuna auction at full swing. Head up to Sarangani Highlands for a scenic breakfast with panoramic bay views.

🌙 Evening

Dinner at Alfonso's for a special occasion, Max's at Robinsons for a classic Filipino feast, or dive into the local food scene at Tiongson Arcade. Cap the night with a craft cocktail at 7 Monkeys.

Casual Eateries & Local Legends

Tiongson Arcade

Lagao District

Local Favorite Budget-Friendly 6:00 PM onwards · Open Late

GenSan's most beloved evening ritual — an open-air street food arcade where the night is built around freshly caught seafood cooked to order. Point at your choice from the display, and minutes later it arrives sizzling. The essentials: grilled tuna belly, pinaputok na tilapia, baked imbaw (shellfish in broth), sizzling bihod (tuna roe), and calamares. Arrive after six, settle in, and let the smoky aroma do the rest.

🚕 Grab · approx. 15 min from property

Must-Try

Grilled tuna belly, pinaputok na tilapia, baked imbaw, bihod

Best Time

Evening onwards for lively atmosphere

📍 Navigate to Arcade

Tambilawan

Halal · Filipino · Inasal

GenSan's first and only Halal-certified restaurant, and a genuine institution. Dine kamayan-style — meals served on banana leaves, eaten with your hands — in open-air traditional nipa huts. The chicken sisig is a city-wide favorite: crunchy, savory, and deeply satisfying. Order the nilagpang (grilled chicken slow-simmered in broth), and finish with their celebrated buko halo-halo, finely shaved and generously layered. Halal throughout.

In Case of an Emergency

A simple, calm guide for earthquakes and other natural events. You are in safe hands.

1

During the shaking — Drop, Cover, Hold On

Drop to the floor, take cover under a sturdy table or desk, and hold on until the shaking stops. Stay clear of windows, mirrors, and tall furniture. If you are in bed, stay there and shield your head with a pillow. Please don’t run outside while the ground is still moving.

2

Right after — pause and check

Take a breath and check yourself and anyone with you. Watch for broken glass — slip on shoes before walking. Aftershocks are normal and may follow, so stay calm and ready.

3

Step outside calmly if it feels unsafe

If you smell gas, see damage, or simply feel safer outside, leave without rushing. Bring your phone, the key, and an ID. Use the stairs, never elevators, and gather in an open space away from buildings, walls, and power lines.

4

Near the coast? Move to higher ground

General Santos sits by Sarangani Bay. If you happen to be near the shoreline and the shaking feels strong or lasts a long time, head inland or uphill right away — don’t wait for an official siren.

5

Power & utilities

If you can do it safely, switch off the unit’s main breaker. Avoid candles in case of a gas leak — use your phone light instead. The in-unit EcoFlow power station can keep your phone charged if electricity is interrupted.

6

Reach us right away

Message your host and our on-the-ground partner immediately. We will guide you step by step and check in on you. You are never on your own here.

For any emergency, please don’t hesitate to contact us or our on-the-ground partner immediately — day or night. The emergency numbers you may need are listed just below.
