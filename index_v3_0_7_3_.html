Gumasa Beach

Glan, Sarangani Province

Boracay of the South White Sand

Six kilometres of powdery white sand facing the Celebes Sea — calm, uncrowded, and genuinely beautiful. Resorts along the strip offer day visits and overnight stays. Best experienced on a dry-season morning before the midday heat.

Getting There

Van from KCC Mall terminal → Glan (~1 hr), then tricycle to beach

Best Time

Feb–April dry season · Morning visits · Last van back by 4 PM

Lake Sebu

South Cotabato Highlands

Seven Falls Zipline T'boli Culture

A highland escape at 1,000m elevation — cool air, mist-covered mountains, and one of Southeast Asia's highest ziplines soaring over seven cascading waterfalls. Add a lake cruise, T'boli Dreamweavers weaving, and fresh tilapia lunch for a genuinely full day.

Getting There

Bus from Bulaong Terminal → ~2 hrs · Habal-habal in Lake Sebu

Don't Miss

Casual Eateries & Local Legends

Tiongson Arcade

Lagao District

Local Favorite Budget-Friendly 6:00 PM onwards · Open Late

GenSan's most beloved evening ritual — an open-air street food arcade where the night is built around freshly caught seafood cooked to order. Point at your choice from the display, and minutes later it arrives sizzling. The essentials: grilled tuna belly, pinaputok na tilapia, baked imbaw (shellfish in broth), sizzling bihod (tuna roe), and calamares. Arrive after six, settle in, and let the smoky aroma do the rest.

🚕 Grab · approx. 15 min from property

Must-Try

Grilled tuna belly, pinaputok na tilapia, baked imbaw, bihod

Best Time

Evening onwards for lively atmosphere

📍 Navigate to Arcade
🍗

Chicken Hauz

Local Institution

A GenSan institution that locals measure all other chicken against. The house specialty — garlic buttered chicken, slow-cooked until the meat pulls effortlessly from the bone, deeply savory and unmistakably aromatic — has drawn devotees across generations. Pair it with kinilaw or pancit canton and a cold mango shake for the full experience. Simple surroundings, honest prices, and a flavor that stays with you.

📍 Find Location →
🍗

Tambilawan

Halal · Filipino · Inasal

GenSan's first and only Halal-certified restaurant, and a genuine institution. Dine kamayan-style — meals served on banana leaves, eaten with your hands — in open-air traditional nipa huts. The chicken sisig is a city-wide favorite: crunchy, savory, and deeply satisfying. Order the nilagpang (grilled chicken slow-simmered in broth), and finish with their celebrated buko halo-halo, finely shaved and generously layered. Halal throughout.
